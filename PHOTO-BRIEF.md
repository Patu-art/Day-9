## Important

- Instagram being publicly viewable **does not mean** the images are licensed for republication. Request permission from the business before uploading their images, logos, and photos of people to a publicly hosted demo. Press photography may be owned by the publication or photographer rather than the business.
- Ideally request original export files from the owner. Avoid blurred screenshots, Google Maps thumbnails, stock bowls falsely represented as the brand's products, and unannounced store shots.
- For the demo, do not invent prices, toppings, bowl variants, events, order capability or launch details.
- Once photos are available, replace the hero illustration and two concept cards with the above image paths, crop with `object-fit: cover`, and use suitable `alt` text.
